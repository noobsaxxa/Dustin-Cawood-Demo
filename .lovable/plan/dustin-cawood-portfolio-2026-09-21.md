# Dustin Cawood Portfolio

## What I’ll build
- A single-page, mobile-responsive portfolio with a deep charcoal studio aesthetic and restrained amber accents.
- A full-width opening section featuring Dustin’s name, role, film-sound tagline, and an understated animated waveform motif.
- About, selected credits, reel/audio, teaching, and contact sections with generous spacing and minimal copy.
- Five cinematic credit tiles for *Lincoln*, *Chasing Ice*, *WALL·E*, *Super 8*, and *Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol*, each showing the requested role and a poster-style placeholder treatment.
- A refined contact form plus direct email, LinkedIn, and IMDb links.

## Visual direction
- Bold condensed display typography paired with a highly readable modern sans serif.
- Near-black and charcoal surfaces, warm amber as the single accent, thin rules, restrained motion, and sharp-to-subtle corner treatments.
- Editorial layouts inspired by film credits and mixing-console interfaces rather than conventional marketing cards.

## Technical details
- Build the page at `/` using the existing React/TanStack setup.
- Define all colors, fonts, spacing accents, and motion centrally in the project design system.
- Add route-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Keep interactions lightweight and accessible, including reduced-motion support and clear form labels.
- Verify the page visually at desktop and mobile sizes and check the core interactions.
